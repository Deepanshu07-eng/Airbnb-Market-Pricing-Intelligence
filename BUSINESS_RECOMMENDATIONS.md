# Business Recommendations

## Airbnb Market & Pricing Intelligence

The recommendations below are based on the descriptive patterns identified in the project analysis. They should be treated as **data-informed directions**, not guaranteed outcomes.

### 1. Use Median Price for Market Benchmarking

Listing prices are strongly right-skewed, so the median provides a more representative benchmark for a typical listing than the mean.

**Recommendation:**  
Hosts and marketplace analysts should compare a listing against the **city-, room-type-, and property-type-specific median price** rather than relying only on the overall average.

---

### 2. Benchmark Pricing Against Comparable Listings

Pricing differs across cities, room types, property types, and guest-capacity segments.

**Recommendation:**  
Hosts should benchmark against listings with similar characteristics instead of applying a single marketplace-wide pricing reference.

---

### 3. Position Larger Listings Around Group Value

The analysis shows a relationship between guest capacity and observed price per accommodated guest.

**Recommendation:**  
Hosts managing higher-capacity properties can emphasize the value provided to groups and families, while comparing their pricing against similarly sized listings.

---

### 4. Use Superhost Metrics as a Performance Benchmark

The analysis observes differences between Superhosts and other hosts across ratings and host-response/acceptance indicators.

**Recommendation:**  
Hosts can use response rate, acceptance rate, and review quality as operational benchmarks when evaluating their listing performance.

> This does not establish that Superhost status itself causes higher ratings or prices.

---

### 5. Focus on Guest Experience Dimensions

The project compares multiple review-score dimensions, including cleanliness and value.

**Recommendation:**  
Hosts should monitor lower-performing review dimensions and use guest feedback to identify specific service-quality improvements.

---

### 6. Consider Location-Specific Positioning

Listing supply is concentrated differently across cities and neighbourhoods.

**Recommendation:**  
Hosts should evaluate their competitive environment at the local-market level rather than treating the entire Airbnb marketplace as one homogeneous market.

---

### 7. Treat Review Activity as Engagement Evidence, Not Revenue

Review volume changes substantially over time.

**Recommendation:**  
Marketplace analysis can use review activity to understand customer-feedback and engagement patterns, but review counts should not be interpreted as actual bookings or revenue.

---

### 8. Use Caution During Cross-City Price Comparisons

The dataset contains prices in local currencies.

**Recommendation:**  
For formal international price benchmarking, currency normalization should be performed before comparing monetary values across cities.

---

## Summary

The most useful actions supported by the analysis are:

1. Benchmark using **median and comparable listings**.
2. Apply **city- and segment-specific pricing analysis**.
3. Position larger properties around **group value**.
4. Monitor **host operational and guest-experience indicators**.
5. Evaluate opportunities at the **local market level**.
6. Keep clear separation between **listing prices, review activity, bookings, and actual revenue**.
